Our Craft

Stitched with Seva,

Every Divya Poshak begins in the lanes of Vrindavan, where our artisan families have dressed Thakur Ji for generations. We use pure silk, velvet and cotton bases, hand-embroidered with zari, gota-patti, kundan and moti — never glued, always stitched.

  • 🌿

    Blessed before dispatch
